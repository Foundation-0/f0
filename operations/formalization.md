# Formalization: choices and checks

**Research checked 19 October 2026. Proposal only; obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and tax advice
before acting.** See [the decision register](README.md#decisions-needed-from-named-people).

## 1. Start with the public purpose

An appropriate purpose to explore with counsel is advancing scientific knowledge and education
through reproducible human-and-AI research, public dissemination, and openly available research tools.
This is a drafting direction, not filing-ready charitable objects. The actual activities, beneficiaries,
publication practices, private benefits and IP arrangements matter more than the word “nonprofit.”

Canada's [research policy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/policies-guidance/policy-statement-029-research-a-charitable-activity.html)
expects a credible research proposal with educational value, a reasonable prospect of advancing
knowledge, public benefit and dissemination. It also addresses commercial/private benefit and IP.
In the US, [IRS Publication 557](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p557) distinguishes scientific
research in the public interest from ordinary commercial testing and development. Public access to
results and resulting IP is relevant. An open-source license alone does not establish charitable status.

For Foundation0, this suggests documenting research questions, reproducible methods, independent
review, public outputs and evidence limits. Keep speculative claims labeled and separate free public
research from commercial product development.

## 2. Compare the routes

| Route | When to investigate it | What it provides | Main limits / checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compatible fiscal sponsor | Funding is near-term; permanent governance and geography remain uncertain. | An existing charitable organization may receive and administer eligible project funds under its oversight. | Not all funders or sponsors accept the project; fees, control, IP, employment, cross-border payments and exit terms need agreement. |
| US state nonprofit + IRS recognition | There is a meaningful US operating base and a strong US funding rationale. | A corporation plus, separately, recognized federal exemption if approved. | State choice, registration, public-charity classification, annual reporting, foreign activity and employment all need review. |
| Canadian federal/provincial nonprofit + CRA registration | Actual Canadian management/operations and Canadian receipting needs justify it. | A legal organization plus, separately, registered-charity status if approved. | Incorporation is not charitable registration. Federal incorporation does not settle all provincial obligations. |
| Two entities | A concrete donor/grant or operational need cannot be met more simply. | Potentially distinct local legal/fundraising homes. | Two boards/compliance systems, related-party transfers and cross-border tax work; not a default startup choice. |

The [Council on Foundations' fiscal-sponsor FAQ](https://cof.org/content/faq-fiscal-sponsors)
describes sponsorship for projects lacking their own exemption, including non-US organizations.
The sponsor must exercise real charitable oversight, rather than simply pass through earmarked money.
See [the sponsor comparison](funding.md#fiscal-sponsorship-is-administration-not-funding).

## 3. Governance before forms

The public charter is a statement of principles, not a substitute for articles, bylaws, board
resolutions and legally accountable governance. Proposed starting controls:

- At least three capable human directors, with a genuinely independent majority where practical;
  separate scientific advisers as useful. This is a governance recommendation, not a universal legal
  minimum. Confirm the requirements for the chosen jurisdiction and charity classification.
- Written conflicts disclosures, recusals and independent approval of related-party transactions and
  reasonable compensation. No founder should approve their own payment or contract alone.
- A budget, delegated spending limits, expense documentation, bank-signing rules and restricted-fund
  accounting. Make a compliance calendar and name a human responsible for each obligation.
- Inventory the legal owner and license of every repo, domain, dataset and pre-existing tool. Decide
  whether Foundation0 needs a license, donation, service agreement or no transfer at all. Existing
  licenses and contributor rights remain in force.
- Record provenance, AI roles and human review; adopt contribution/licensing terms appropriate to
  each repository. Check third-party data and model terms before promising open redistribution.
- Map founder-owned businesses and any shared staff, compute, contracts or IP. Where ASKA or another
  commercial organization overlaps, document separate accounts, cost allocation, fair terms and
  approval by disinterested people. The existence of a relationship is not itself misconduct.

The [IRS Form 1023 FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-1023)
is a starting point for conflicts and private-benefit review. Canada's federal nonprofit
[director guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/not-profit-corporations/directors)
requires directors to be individuals aged at least 18. Agents can research, propose and operate within
documented limits; they do not replace the responsible human directors and signatories.

## 4. US route: state incorporation and federal exemption are separate

Typical work order, to confirm with counsel:

1. Choose the state based on actual operations, directors, registered-agent requirements and cost.
   Do not default to a state because it is popular for venture-backed companies.
2. Incorporate with appropriate charitable-purpose and dissolution provisions, then adopt bylaws,
   appoint officers and approve initial resolutions and controls.
3. Obtain an EIN, establish authorized financial administration, and prepare an activities narrative,
   financial projections, compensation/conflict disclosures and IP arrangements.
4. Apply for recognition under section 501(c)(3), using the appropriate form. State nonprofit status
   [does not automatically give federal exemption](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/federal-tax-obligations-of-nonprofit-corporations).
5. Address state tax exemption, foreign qualification and
   [charitable-solicitation registrations](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/charitable-solicitation-state-requirements)
   where applicable. These are separate from IRS recognition.

### Federal application cost and timing snapshot

- [IRS fees](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-and-1023-ez-amount-of-user-fee):
  Form 1023 **US$600**; Form 1023-EZ **US$275**. EIN issuance from the IRS is free. State formation,
  registered agents, professional advice, accounting and ongoing compliance cost extra.
- [1023-EZ instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez): generally no annual gross receipts
  over US$50,000 in the relevant past/projected years and assets no more than US$250,000, plus every
  other eligibility-workbook condition. Foreign incorporation or a foreign-country mailing address
  disqualifies an applicant. Being a scientific organization alone is not a disqualification.
- The contemplated grant sizes, cross-border activities, IP and commercial overlaps make the full
  application worth assessing; do not choose EZ merely to save fees. A single US$90,000 projected
  award, for example, would exceed the EZ receipts threshold for that projected year.
- The [IRS processing page](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/wheres-my-application-for-tax-exempt-status)
  published 80% determination metrics of **191 days** for full 1023, **22 days** for EZ and **120 days**
  for EZ cases requiring additional review when checked. These are published metrics, not a guaranteed
  turnaround; queue dates and conditions change. Recheck before setting a funding timetable.

“Foundation” in the name does not select private-foundation tax treatment. Evaluate the applicable
[public-charity/private-foundation classification and support tests](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/determine-your-foundation-classification).
Calendar obligations from formation: applicable Form 990-series returns/notices may be due while an
exemption application is pending or before it is submitted. Also plan for state returns/renewals,
public disclosure, donor acknowledgments, employment/tax obligations and grant reports. An entity with
little activity can still have filing duties. See the [1023-EZ annual-filing instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez)
and [IRS charity lifecycle](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/life-cycle-of-a-public-charity).

## 5. Canadian route: incorporation and registered-charity status are separate

Choose federal or provincial incorporation in light of actual operations. A typical sequence is to
settle charitable purposes with counsel, incorporate, adopt bylaws and initial resolutions, establish
financial controls, and separately apply to CRA with an activities plan, budget and governing documents.

The [federal nonprofit overview](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/not-profit-corporations)
and [fee schedule](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/services-fees-and-processing-times)
currently list online incorporation at **C$200**, with a **one-business-day** service standard, and an
online annual return at **C$12**. These figures exclude advice and other registrations. Provincial or
territorial registration obligations still need checking.

[CRA's comparison](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/about-registered-charities/what-difference-between-a-registered-charity-a-non-profit-organization.html)
draws a crucial distinction: registered charities may issue official donation receipts; ordinary NPOs
cannot. An organization operating exclusively as a charity cannot simply choose ordinary NPO treatment.
Do not equate a not-for-profit incorporation certificate with CRA registration or tax receipting authority.

For a body conducting its own research, assess the “charitable organization” designation rather than
assuming it must be a foundation. [CRA designations](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/registering-charitable-qualified-donee-status/apply-become-registered-charity/establishing/types-registered-charities-designations.html)
depend on activities, funding and governance; charitable organizations and public foundations require
more than half of directors to deal with one another at arm's length.

CRA's [2026–27 service standard](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/service-standards-cra/service-standards-2026-27.html)
targets a final registration decision within **nine months of a complete application**, 80% of the time.
This is not an incorporation timeline or an approval guarantee. After registration, budget for T3010
reporting, proper receipts and books, applicable disbursement requirements, corporate annual returns,
employment obligations and provincial compliance. A corporate annual return and a charity return are
different filings.

## 6. Cross-border giving and work

Neither country's charity status automatically gives the other country's residents ordinary donation
deductions or credits. Relief under [Canada–US treaty Article XXI](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/tax-policy/tax-treaties/country/united-states-america-convention-consolidated-1980-1983-1984-1995-1997.html)
generally depends on relevant cross-border income and exceptions; the donor's specific situation
matters. See also [CRA's gifts guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/p113/p113-gifts-income-tax.html).
Do not advertise donations as deductible in both countries by default.

A US sponsor is not automatically a Canadian receipting solution. A Canadian charity cannot merely
lend its registration number: [CRA's grants-to-non-qualified-donees guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/charities/policies-guidance/charities-making-grants-non-qualified-donees.html)
sets accountability requirements for grants; running its own activities through an intermediary is
a different arrangement with its own rules. Ask counsel to select the actual structure.

Separately check employment/contractor classification, payroll, withholding, where work is performed,
cross-border payments, sanctions, privacy, insurance and ownership of commissioned work. Do not assume
a worldwide grant call resolves these questions.

## 7. Counsel-ready packet and acceptance gate

Before paying for filings, assemble F1–F7 from the [decision register](README.md), a six- to
twelve-month activities plan, three-year projections if required, willing directors and relationship
disclosures, IP inventory, proposed compensation/contracts, donor geography and target grant rules.
Ask counsel to return: the recommended jurisdiction and reasons, filing sequence and all-in budget,
charitable purposes, governance documents, cross-border analysis, compliance calendar and matters that
require founder decisions.

No incorporation, tax election, charity application, IP transfer, sponsor agreement, bank account or
fundraising representation is approved by this document. Evidence of completion must be actual records
reviewed by an authorized person, not a checked box added by an agent.
