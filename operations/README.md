# Formalization and funding roadmap

**Status: research proposal, 19 October 2026. Not adopted policy or legal advice.**

This is a working packet for the founding members to review and improve in GitHub. It does not
establish that Foundation0 is incorporated, a registered charity, tax-exempt, or eligible for a grant.
Those facts have not been established here. It makes no choice of country or state, appoints nobody,
and authorizes no filing, fundraising, application, contract, expenditure, or change to repository access.

The [charter](../docs/charter/index.html#principles) already supplies the right starting points:
public work, named human accountability, credit for people and machines, evidence labels, and free
public-benefit tools. Legal governing documents and a funded operating plan are separate work.

## Read the packet

- [Formalization](formalization.md): sponsor-first, US, and Canadian routes; governance, IP,
  cross-border limits, indicative fees and ongoing obligations.
- [Funding pipeline](funding.md): ranked opportunities, current availability, eligibility gaps,
  fiscal sponsors, restrictions, and the material terms to review before submitting.
- [Candidate first project](verification-pilot.md): a reviewable six-month verification pilot,
  deliverables, evaluation plan, and an illustrative US$90,000 budget. This is an option, not an
  application or a commitment by any maintainer.

These files live outside `docs/`, like `deep-fields/`. They are repository working material and are
not a new page on the published site. Existing site and research claims are unchanged.

## Provisional recommendation

Prepare one narrow, costed public-good project while checking whether a compatible fiscal sponsor
would help. Do not wait for a new corporation to start preparing the AI for Math seed proposal:
its current rules explicitly welcome independent teams. Select a permanent jurisdiction only after
the actual operating locations, donor base, governance, and administrative cost are known. Avoid
creating two entities solely because contributors are in two countries.

This recommendation is an inference from the sources in the linked notes, not a founder decision
or a finding that any sponsor will accept Foundation0.

## Suggested sequence after review

The intervals below are planning targets measured from adoption, not promised external turnaround.
No person has been assigned work by this proposal.

| Interval | Work | Evidence needed to move on |
|---|---|---|
| First week | Resolve F1–F4; inventory IP and commercial overlaps; choose one candidate project. | Named lead, project scope, rough budget, legal-status statement, decisions linked. |
| Weeks 2–3 | Finish the project brief and evidence pack; compare sponsor terms with the specific funder's rules; obtain formation advice. | Rights inventory, milestones, cost basis, grantee/admin route, overhead compatibility. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Human review of a seed proposal; consider a Sloan inquiry after confirming eligible grantee arrangements. | Approved text, budget, signatory, privacy/terms review, explicit submission authority. |
| Months 2–3 | If justified, establish the chosen entity and compliance calendar; otherwise operate under an approved sponsor agreement. | Actual incorporation/sponsorship records, controls, accounts and documented responsibilities. |
| At first award / quarterly | Track restricted funds, publish appropriate progress and evidence, review runway and jurisdiction assumptions. | Budget-to-actuals, scientific outcomes, reporting deadlines, public corrections. |

Incorporation and charity applications can run far longer than this preparation schedule. Do not
describe a pending application as approval or promise donors tax treatment before it is verified.

## How to maintain this packet

- Recheck program pages before outreach, submission or any claim that a call is open. Each funding
  entry carries its last-check date and next evidence needed.
- Keep recommendations, eligibility questions, human decisions and completed actions distinct.
- Link changes to primary sources and keep corrections in Git history. Do not silently upgrade a
  proposed project to an adopted policy or a claimed result to an independently verified one.
- Never place confidential proposals, donor records, application credentials or directors' private
  details in this public repository.
