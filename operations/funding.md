## 1. AI for Math Fund seed grants

Primary sources: [official fund page](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/ai-for-math-fund/)
and the linked [April 2026 seed RFP and FAQ](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WOTctEoNMOypKb3XB01pldDPHo2wN2ZFhZhjkXwOCc0/edit?tab=t.0).

The seed RFP allows individuals, independent teams, nonprofits and other organizations worldwide,
subject to sanctions/compliance limits; no institutional affiliation is required. It offers up to
US$100,000 for up to 12 months on a rolling basis. It states a four-week response target, not an
approval guarantee. The separate US$100,000–US$1 million annual round is closed for 2026.

Relevant themes include early prototypes, datasets/benchmarks, and tools connecting AI research and
proof infrastructure. Personnel, contractors and cloud compute can be project costs. Indirects are
capped at **10% of the total award**, for all applicants. Every expense must serve the project.

Before submission: approve the human lead, realistic time commitments, deliverables, cost basis,
administrative recipient and budget. The [candidate pilot](verification-pilot.md) is a starting point
for review. Verify payment/tax arrangements and the treatment of any sponsor fee rather than assuming
incorporation or a sponsor is mandatory.

Material terms: funded code must be open source; funded datasets shared in an open-access repository
with documentation; research shared through
the required open-access/preprint route; funding acknowledged. Creator IP remains subject to release
requirements. Submitted materials are generally **not confidential**, and the RFP includes a release
of liability. Review the complete current terms before authorizing submission; exclude proprietary,
personal and third-party confidential information. A public draft is not acceptance of these terms.

## 2. Alfred P. Sloan Foundation: Open Source in Science

Primary sources: [program](https://sloan.org/programs/digital-technology/open-source-in-science)
and [application process](https://sloan.org/grants/apply).

The program funds the practices and institutions behind discovery-enabling open software, hardware
and models. Its stated interests include institutional support, publication/archiving, stewardship,
career roles and human-AI collaboration in scientific software. A maintained verification tool with
external users and clear reusable outputs is a stronger fit than an undefined request to fund a new
foundation.

The program emphasizes tooling, institutions, economic models and incentives over individual
open-source development projects. An inquiry should therefore demonstrate ecosystem/practices,
archiving or stewardship benefits beyond maintaining one package; fit remains to be assessed.

The program invites a two-page letter of inquiry; full proposals require invitation. The general
process is year-round and allows up to eight weeks for inquiry responses. Sloan normally funds
organizations, not individuals. The public program page does not establish blanket fiscal-sponsor
acceptance: confirm the actual eligible grantee arrangement before drafting as if it were settled.

Proposed inquiry outline, after approval: scientific users and unmet need; evidence from current
repositories; the proposed software and maintenance plan; measurable adoption/reproducibility goals;
team and governance; requested project amount and duration; legal grantee/admin route. No amount or
award expectation is established here. Treat this as restricted project funding unless agreed otherwise.

## 3. Simons: watch for the actual call

Primary source: [15 October 2026 announcement](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/news/simons-foundation-launches-15m-effort-to-help-mathematicians-navigate-ai-era/).

Simons, Anthropic and OpenAI announced US$15 million in combined funding/resources, including an open
call to support mathematics-community work such as evaluating AI-assisted results, learning tools,
workshops and shared standards. This fits Foundation0's verification and human-accountability aims.

The announcement does **not** publish application dates, detailed eligibility, award sizes, sponsor
rules or PI qualifications. Keep this on the watchlist until a real solicitation exists. A qualified
mathematician collaborator may strengthen a project, but no academic-affiliation requirement should
be invented. Do not represent the US$15 million as a grant available to Foundation0.

Sources: [CS&S project intake](https://www.codeforsociety.org/become-a-fiscally-sponsored-project),
[CS&S FAQ](https://www.codeforsociety.org/fsp/faq),
[NumFOCUS project page](https://numfocus.org/projects-overview). Last checked 19 October 2026.
No acceptance, capacity or country-specific service availability has been confirmed with either.
The CS&S FAQ returned HTTP 500 in final direct-link validation; the figures above were also checked
against its search-indexed text. Treat them as a research snapshot and confirm the current agreement,
including any minimum revenue requirement, rather than a live quotation of offered terms.

**Budget conflict to resolve:** a 15% sponsor fee may not fit a grant's 10%-of-total indirect cap.
For an illustrative US$90,000 award, those percentages are US$13,500 and US$9,000 respectively, a
US$4,500 gap if the entire fee counts as indirect. Ask the funder and sponsor how the fee is classified
and whether terms can accommodate it. Do not relabel administrative overhead as direct research cost
to bypass the cap. An approved fee change, a permitted separate funding source, another admin route
or a different grant may be necessary.

Sponsorship should be compared on oversight/control, financial and employment services, full costs,
publication/IP rights, fundraising representations, cross-border support, termination and the handling
of remaining restricted funds. A US sponsor does not automatically solve Canadian donor receipting;
see [cross-border limits](formalization.md#6-cross-border-giving-and-work).

## Secondary and deferred routes

- [Mitacs Accelerate](https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/accelerate/) is a Canadian research
  collaboration route requiring academic participation, eligible nonacademic partners and matching
  contributions. It is institution-administered research staffing support, not unrestricted nonprofit
  startup money. Confirm the specific partners and current terms before proposing it.
- [NSF CSSI](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/cssi-cyberinfrastructure-systems-scientific-innovation)
  currently says it is waiting for a new publication, with no upcoming deadlines. Do not list it as an
  open call. Reassess only when a solicitation establishes an eligible fit.
- [Grants.gov applicant setup](https://www.grants.gov/quick-start-guide/applicants) generally requires
  organizational applicants to obtain a UEI and active SAM registration. Registration does not itself
  establish eligibility. Defer this administrative overhead until a specific call justifies it.
- Donations, in-kind compute and corporate support may diversify funding, but no specific donor,
  credit program or amount has been validated here. Keep recognition terms, vendor independence and
  public access intact; disclose conflicts and restrictions. Do not build the cash runway around
  speculative credits or uncommitted volunteer time.

## Shared preparation packet

Before any outreach or submission, prepare:

1. A one-page public-benefit explanation, a named human lead, and the precise project scope.
2. Reproducible existing examples, their true evidence labels, prior work and a clear account of what
   the new funding buys. Separate original work from contributions to other people's projects.
3. Milestones, success/failure measures, domain review, rights/licenses and a publication plan.
4. A justified budget with quantities/rates, personnel time, compute caps, allowed overhead and
   reporting responsibilities. Separate restricted project costs from core operations.
5. A verified legal/admin recipient, conflicts disclosures, signatory and funder/sponsor compatibility.
6. Human review of data disclosures, terms, public statements and the exact final submission.

For each pursued opportunity, record: human owner, source and recheck date, applicant type, deadline
or rolling status, requested amount, indirect basis, terms, open questions, approval link, submission
date and reporting dates. Mark “not submitted” until there is real submission evidence. A merged PR
does not submit an application or authorize an agent to sign for Foundation0.
