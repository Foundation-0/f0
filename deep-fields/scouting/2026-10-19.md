# Second pass, 19 October 2026

Five research agents scouted again, one day after the first pass: mathematics and theoretical computer science;
physics, chemistry and Earth science; astronomy; life sciences, metascience and the humanities; and checking what AI is
claimed to have discovered. They built on the first pass rather than repeating it, and checked whether its leads were
still open. A sixth agent then checked 15 load-bearing facts against their sources. All 15 held, and three were
reworded. The founding members created a repository for each project below the same day.

Each repository's `notes/scouting-2026-10-19.md` holds that project's findings in full, with every source, split into
facts checked on 19 October and facts from the scouting reports that were not re-checked.

## What changed since the first pass

- **Checking AI claims is the gap.** Cataloguing AI contributions is now crowded, with parallel registries and a Lean
  registry. Doing the checks (building proofs, comparing formal statements with the papers, re-running analyses) is
  not. On 15 October the Simons Foundation, Anthropic and OpenAI announced $15 million for workshops and tools to help
  mathematicians evaluate AI-assisted results, so others will follow.
- **Gaia DR4 is more crowded than the first pass said.** Gaia's own team is checking its 1,007 transit candidates
  against TESS. The gaia-dr4 project checks other claims instead.
- **Three first-pass leads are still open:** codes over GF(3) to GF(9), OEIS "empirical" recurrences (one new entrant
  posted Lean proofs on 12 October), and circulant weighing matrices.

## Set aside

- **Re-running the claims of automated-scientist systems, AI-found cryptanalysis, and two kinds of health-data papers**
  (single-factor NHANES associations, Mendelian randomization). Each means telling named authors or labs their result
  doesn't reproduce, and needs specialists. Revisit later.
- **Lower significance or long odds:** missed symmetry in the Crystallography Open Database, the rectilinear crossing
  number of K₂₈, and the ML-validated planets audit as a project of its own (it is folded into gaia-dr4).
- **Crowded since the first pass:** Hadamard matrices below order 2000, Legendre pairs, snake-in-the-box,
  no-three-in-line, Kobon triangles, the Thomson problem for small N, tables of absolutely maximally entangled states,
  Costas arrays, AI-discovered materials, reproducing machine-learning papers and social-science replication packages,
  fabricated references, Rubin (its data are restricted for two years), SPHEREx and Euclid.
