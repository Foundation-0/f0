## 2. Four-Gate Anchored Conservation Quotient (`ACQ`) Pattern

They are not audits of those repositories' data or code. `PROVEN` below means a Lean 4 theorem over `Int` or `Nat` that compiles on the pinned toolchain. Those theorems are arithmetic identities and do not model AUROC, assays, search graphs or floating-point code.

The dust witness thresholds (`1/1000` and `5/2`) are typed in as well.

The four gates:

1. **Gate 1: Independent `c = 0` Control & Shift-Rank Anchor:** Takes the channel bleed `alpha` from a separate control specimen with known conversion `c = 0` (for example the `p = 0` recombinant paralog control named in `Foundation-0/project-asma#82`), read on the same channel as the sample, then deflates `q_corr(p) = (y(p) - alpha) / (1 - alpha)`. The kernel checks that declared provenance only. It cannot tell whether the named control really has `c = 0`, and that fact has to come from the assay. Gate 1 also normalizes multi-shard evaluations to within-shard empirical mid-ranks, removing additive shard offsets. Status: the algebra of both pieces is `PROVEN` (arithmetic lemma) in `AnchoredConservationQuotient.lean::within_shard_shift_invariant` and `pgl2_bleed_isomorphism`.
